## Detailed E-E-A-T Assessment

### Experience — 11/25

**What's present:**
- Three case studies are referenced (Marketpl7ce ad operations platform, Paynest financial OS, LinkedIn Agent)
- A 4-step execution methodology is documented (Diagnose → Architect → Deploy → Optimize)
- The comparison table showing Monjez vs. Generic Agency vs. DIY demonstrates operational awareness

**What's missing:**
- Case study pages are referenced but linked only to a "Show Project" CTA — case study detail pages would dramatically increase experience signals
- Zero specific, quantified results in visible content (e.g., "reduced manual workload by 73% for Client X")
- The metrics section ("0+ PROJECTS DELIVERED", "0% WORKFLOW AUTOMATED") renders as literal zeros to search crawlers due to JavaScript animation — this is the single most damaging content issue on the page
- No before/after documentation, screenshots of real systems, or client-generated data
- The ROI calculator uses generic defaults ($60K salary, 30% automation potential) with no client-validated benchmark sources

**Recommendation:** Transform the 3 case study references into full case study pages. Each should include: client background, specific problem, solution architecture, measurable result (e.g., "62% reduction in manual task time"), and a client quote.

### Expertise — 16/25

**What's present:**
- Strong technical vocabulary throughout: "LLMs, vector databases, integration points," "Headless architecture," "deep API & database level," "autonomous agents execution"
- Technology partner logos listed (OpenAI, Meta Ads, Google Cloud, Shopify, Stripe, Supabase, AWS, Make.com, HubSpot) — shows breadth of stack knowledge
- Three distinct service verticals defined with clear sub-components (Ad Tech Automation, CRM Intelligence, Conversion Optimization, etc.)

**What's missing:**
- No named team members, founders, or engineers anywhere on the page — expertise claims are anonymous
- No certifications, partnerships (official AWS partner, OpenAI partner, etc.) or credentials
- No author attribution for the case studies or methodology
- No educational content demonstrating expertise (blog, guides, whitepapers)

**Recommendation:** Add a "Team" or "About" section with at minimum the founder's name, background, and LinkedIn. Even a 2-sentence bio per key person dramatically improves Expertise signals. Pair this with an `/about` page.

### Authoritativeness — 8/25

This is the lowest-scoring dimension and the one with the biggest gap.

**What's present:**
- Technology integration logos suggest ecosystem credibility
- "Boutique Execution Model" framing positions as selective/elite
- The comparison table establishes positioning vs. generic agencies

**What's missing:**
- No named clients (even with permission) or client logos
- No press mentions, media coverage, or "As seen in" section
- No third-party directory listings, awards, or recognitions (Clutch, G2, etc.)
- No LinkedIn company page referenced
- No social proof numbers (e.g., "4.9/5 from 12 clients on Clutch")
- The site is not indexed by Google, meaning no external sites are linking to it — zero domain authority signals

**Recommendation:** This is the #1 E-E-A-T gap. Priority actions:
1. Add 2-3 client logos with permission (even small clients build authority)
2. Create a Clutch.co or G2 profile and request reviews
3. Add a "Featured In" or "Trusted By" section once any press coverage exists
4. Start publishing case studies as link-worthy assets

## AI Content Assessment

**Overall assessment: Authentic human expertise with AI-generation risk signals**

**Positive signals:**
- Specific, technical framing suggests genuine domain knowledge
- The "boutique" positioning and explicit client cap are distinctive, non-generic messaging
- Pricing specificity ($2,000, $300/month) is unusual for AI-generated content
- The comparison table with specific feature dimensions shows product thinking

**Risk signals:**
- Several sections use highly structured, parallel phrasing that could read as AI-assisted: "Strategic AI Architecture... End-to-End System Design... Continuous Optimization..." (all 3-word feature titles with identical grammatical structure)
- "We don't sell 'chatbots'. We build full operational ecosystems where data flows like electricity" — vivid and original, but stylistically consistent with AI-assisted copywriting
- No dates, no personal anecdotes, no first-person voice from a named author

**Verdict:** Content quality is above baseline but lacks the unmistakably human E-E-A-T signals that Google's Quality Raters and AI citation systems prioritize. Adding named voices (founder quotes, engineer perspectives) would differentiate this immediately.
